## Assessment

Choose one of two coding projects: a moving contact line or van der Waals film rupture. The code submission contributes 40% and the final presentation and defence contribute 60%. AI use is encouraged and must be documented in an AI log.

The [[Lecture-Notes/Intro-Soft-Matter/2026/Assessment|assessment page]] gives both problems, the questions, the dates and the slides. The [2026 course repository](https://github.com/comphy-lab/Intro-Soft-Matter-2026) is where you submit, and its README has the full brief and pull-request instructions. This assessment replaces the paper-led presentations used in 2025.
